Sunset Vice is a top-down open-world crime game set in Palmetto Bay, a fictional neon city of beaches, boulevards and back alleys. Explore the city on foot or by car, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, take on story missions, fight back, and build your reputation. Everything in it is original: the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound and music are made for this game. It runs in a desktop browser from static files, offline, with keyboard and mouse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to enter or exit a car, Space to punch, mouse to aim and click to shoot, P or Esc to pause, M to mute. Your money, completed missions and best score save in the browser.
